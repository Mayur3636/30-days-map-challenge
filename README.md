30-Day Map Challenge (2025)
This repository contains a consolidated PDF showcasing all 30 thematic maps developed for the #30DayMapChallenge, demonstrating advanced competencies in GIScience, Remote Sensing, Earth Observation analytics, and geospatial cartography. Each map was produced through a rigorous workflow integrating data sourcing, geospatial preprocessing, spatial modelling, and visualization design.
The collection spans domains such as environmental monitoring, climate analytics, energy systems, disaster assessment, mobility, and urban planning, reflecting the practical application of modern geospatial technologies in decision-support contexts.

The challenge involved the use of multi-resolution datasets (Sentinel-1/2, MODIS, USGS, Living Atlas, OSM, NREL, GOES) and analytical platforms including Google Earth Engine, ArcGIS Pro/Online, QGIS, and Python geospatial libraries (GeoPandas, Rasterio, Plotly, Folium). Emphasis was placed on producing technically accurate, interpretable, and visually coherent spatial outputs aligned with contemporary cartographic standards.

Technical Highlights
Integration of multi-source EO datasets for spatial modelling and thematic analysis

Implementation of workflows such as SAR-based change detection, land cover classification, flood & landslide mapping, spatial statistics, and network-based visualization

Application of advanced cartographic design principles for clarity, aesthetics, and analytical relevance

Demonstrated consistency in executing full geospatial pipelines across 30 independent themes

Showcases capability to translate geospatial datasets into actionable insights for planning, research, and policy contexts
